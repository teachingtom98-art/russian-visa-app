Before You Submit

What physical documents do you need to bring to the appointment?

When attending your appointment at the Russian Visa Application Centre (e.g. Russia Visa Centre / VFS Global) or Russian Consular Section, bring:

  • Your original signed passport (valid 6+ months past visa expiry)
  • One printed, dated, and signed Electronic Visa Application (EVA) form from visa.kdmid.ru
  • One standard passport photo (35mm × 45mm) glued to the designated box on the EVA form
  • Original Tourist Voucher / Confirmation or Official Invitation (MVD/MID)
  • Travel medical insurance certificate (minimum €30,000 cover for the Russian Federation)
Processing times, consular fees, and expedited rush service

Standard processing typically takes 4 to 20 working days depending on your nationality and consular workload.

Express expedited processing (1 to 3 working days) is available for selected visa categories upon payment of an expedited consular surcharge.
